Board of Directors

The Board of Directors of Grameen Bank consists of 13 directors. Among them, three directors, including the Chairman of the Board are appointed by the Government, and the 9 directors are elected from among the shareholder borrower members of Grameen Bank. The remaining director is the managing director as ex-officio of the bank.

SARDER AKHTER HAMED

Managing Director of Grameen Bank

View Details →

Sarder Akhter Hamed has been appointed as the Managing Director of Grameen Bank, says a statement. Prior to His new role, He served as the Chief Operting Officer at sajida foundation. Akhter Hamed, Who did his Master’s In Finance and Banking from Dhaka University, started off his career at British American Tobacco in 1995. In 2005, He joined BRAC Bank Limited. In 2015, He joined Bank Asia as Senior Executive Vice President. He was promoted to Deputy Managing Director in 2021. Mr. Hamed has extensive experience in managing MSME, retail, agriculture and microfinance channel banking in various banks. He has been Instrumental In expanding business opertions.

~Key Highlights of His Expertise~•

 

Global Expansion Leadership: Successfully crafted and executed strategies that opened new markets, resulting in significant revenue growth and business expansion.

.• Digital Transformation: Pioneered initiatives that integrated cutting-edge technologies into core banking functions, improving operational speed, accuracy, and security.

.• Strategic Growth and Sustainability: Focused on aligning operations with long-term business goals to ensure continuousgrowth and scalability in a competitive landscape.

.• Product Innovation: Led the launch of financial products designed to capture market share and foster long-term client loyalty.

A bank for the poor. Grameen Bank and Prof. Muhammad Yunus won Nobel Peace Prize in 2006. The Bank has disbursed collateral free loan of $ 42.49 billion to around 10.63 million borrowers.
